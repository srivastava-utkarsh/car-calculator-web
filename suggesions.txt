Visual & Design Improvements

Alignment & Balance

Right now, blocks feel stacked and heavy. Try making them horizontal cards (side-by-side) if it’s going on a website header, or keep vertical but with equal spacing.

Color consistency

The green, blue, and brown mix feels a bit random.

Suggestion: Use a consistent palette (e.g., gradient blues with icons, or traffic-light colors → green for downpayment, orange for loan term, red for EMI cap).

Icons / Visual cues

Add small icons for quick recognition:

💰 for down payment

⏳ for loan tenure

📊 or 💵 for income rule

Hierarchy

Highlight the numbers (20 / 4 / 10) visually (big bold text) so the user instantly sees the rule. Example:

“20%” in bold green

“4 Years” in bold blue

“10% Income” in bold orange

Background

Dark gradient is nice, but text contrast could be improved. White or light text with slightly larger font size would make it more readable.
